## Current Research

I am currently conducting research on generative diffusion models at SAMM, Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, under the supervision of Prof. Alain Celisse.

My research examines:

* Convergence rates of Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models
* Memorization in score matching
* Generalization from empirical distributions
* Intrinsic low-dimensional data structures
* Asymptotic consistency of generative models
* Tikhonov regularization
* Neural network under-parameterization
* Early stopping
* Theoretical and empirical evaluation of diffusion models

A central part of my work is understanding why diffusion models memorize training data and how regularization methods can improve their generalization behavior.
